About Ashtavinayak Darshan

Ashtavinayak Darshan is a multilingual tourism guide to the eight Ganesha temples of Maharashtra. It was created as a college portfolio project to help pilgrims and travellers plan a respectful, comfortable yatra.

Every page can be read in English, मराठी or हिंदी. Your language choice is remembered on this device.

What you can do here

  • Read the history, legend and architecture of each temple.
  • Find temples and places by name in any of the three languages.
  • Compare routes from Pune, Mumbai, Ahilyanagar or Nashik.
  • Create a day-wise itinerary and print it.

Festival information

Dates follow the Hindu lunar calendar, so they change every year.

Ganesh Chaturthi

Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi (usually August–September). The biggest festival at all eight temples.

Ganesh Jayanti

Magh Shukla Chaturthi (usually January–February). Celebrated as the birth day of Ganesha.

Sankashti Chaturthi

The fourth day after the full moon, every month. Devotees fast and visit the temples in the evening.

Angarki Chaturthi

A Sankashti Chaturthi that falls on a Tuesday is considered especially auspicious.

Temple etiquette

  • Dress modestly and cover shoulders and knees.
  • Remove footwear at the designated place before entering.
  • Speak softly, wait in the queue and do not push.
  • Follow photography rules, especially near the sanctum.
  • Offer flowers, durva grass or modak, and avoid plastic bags.
  • Keep the premises clean and do not litter.
